# About

Clauseway is a logic programming stack for Java: relations, constraints
and tabling over real data. It is developed in the open at
[github.com/clauseway](https://github.com/clauseway), and this site
carries the [blog](blog/index.md) and the [tutorial](tutorial/index.md).

## Contact

Write to [hello@clauseway.org](mailto:hello@clauseway.org) with
questions, with corrections to anything published here, or to say where
the engine fell short on your domain. That last kind of message is the
most useful one.

A bug with a reproduction is best filed as an issue on the repository it
belongs to: [functional](https://github.com/clauseway/functional/issues),
[logic](https://github.com/clauseway/logic/issues) or
[pldb](https://github.com/clauseway/pldb/issues).
